# Contributing

This repository collects real questions, concerns, risks, and challenges
experienced by people doing business with China.

Contributions are welcome.

You can contribute:

- A question you have encountered
- A sourcing problem
- A supplier verification concern
- A factory inspection problem
- A quality-control issue
- A shipping or customs problem
- A payment concern
- A manufacturing problem
- A communication problem
- A local business challenge in China

Please describe the problem clearly and anonymously when appropriate.

Do not submit private information, passwords, payment credentials,
personal identification documents, or confidential business information.
